Privacy Policy

PPR Live app & Primetime Power Radio live player

The short version PPR Live lets you listen to Primetime Power Radio live. We do not ask for, collect, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the PPR Live Android app (package com.primetimepowershow.twa) and the Primetime Power Radio web player at primetimepowershow.com (together, the "App") handle information. The App is provided by Primetime Power Radio ("we," "us," or "our").

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details to use it.

How the live stream works

When you press Play, your device connects to our streaming server to receive audio. Like any internet connection, this exchange includes technical information your device sends automatically, such as your IP address and device/browser type. Our streaming provider uses this only to deliver the audio and to produce an anonymous count of how many people are listening at once (the "listening now" number). We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with any other information.

The App also checks our streaming server every few seconds for the current program title and listener count. These requests contain no personal information.

Information stored on your device

  • Volume setting – your preferred volume level is saved on your device so it is remembered next time. It never leaves your device.
  • App files – the App saves a copy of its own screens and images on your device so it opens quickly. Audio is never saved or recorded.

You can remove all of this at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, files, or other personal data on your device. It uses an internet connection to play the stream.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Google Chrome: on Android, the App displays our player using Google Chrome. Chrome's own handling of data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: the player loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives standard connection information (such as IP address) when the font is downloaded.
  • Links: the App includes links to our website and to Facebook, X, Instagram, and Threads. If you follow those links, you leave the App, and those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience radio player. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to retain or delete about you. Information stored on your device (described above) is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Security

The App connects to our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
